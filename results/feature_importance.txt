Feature Importance Analysis

The TF-IDF representation showed that specific Arabic writing patterns contributed strongly to classification performance.

Linear SVM achieved the highest ROC-AUC score and demonstrated stable separation between AI-generated and human-written Arabic text.

Important indicators included:
- Repeated stylistic structures
- Consistent sentence lengths
- Repetitive token patterns
- Low linguistic variability in AI-generated content

Human-written text showed higher lexical diversity and more variation in sentence structure.
